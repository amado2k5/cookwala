Cookwala / AI assistants

Ask Cookwala from your AI assistant

One URL gives an assistant the Cookwala recipe catalog: search by cuisine, ingredient, nutrition, cost, method, diet, allergen and language, scale a recipe, plan a day, build a shopping list. It is read-only, needs no account and no token.

The URL

https://mcp.cookwala.ai/open/mcp (MCP, streamable HTTP, 24 read-only tools). For tools that take an OpenAPI description: https://mcp.cookwala.ai/api/openapi.json.

Where it works

AssistantHow to add it
ChatGPTA custom GPT that imports the OpenAPI URL above (no authentication), or the MCP URL where your plan offers connectors. Step by step
ClaudeSettings, Connectors, Add custom connector, paste the MCP URL. Or run npx -y @cookwala/mcp locally. Details
GeminiSettings, Connected apps, Add a custom app, paste the MCP URL (where your account and region offer custom apps). On a phone
Copilot Studio, Perplexity, Cursor, VS Code and othersAdd a remote MCP server or custom connector with the MCP URL. MCP reference

Things to try

  • "How do I cook béchamel?"
  • "High-protein vegetarian dinners under 500 kcal."
  • "What can I make with lentils, rice and onions?"
  • "Recipes with no allergens found that are diabetic-friendly."
  • "Plan a 1,800 kcal day without dairy, with a shopping list."

Please know

Allergen, diet and diabetic labels are screens from ingredient names, not guarantees and not medical advice; nutrition and cost are estimates. Read the terms and the privacy policy. Help and bug reports: GitHub issues.
