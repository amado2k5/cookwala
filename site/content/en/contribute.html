Most ways to help need no code

Cookwala is maintained in public. Every contribution is credited in the changelog; every change to the standard goes through an RFC with a public comment period. There is no membership and no fee.

How decisions are made

Small fixes: a pull request and one maintainer. Vocabulary entries and vendor namespaces: a pull request, 7 days. Spec changes: an RFC, 30 days of public comment, a decision with written reasons. Safety-relevant changes also need a qualified reviewer. A steering committee with seats for device makers, food banks, dietetics, privacy, a low- or middle-income country and labour takes over once there are independent adopters, under published numbers. Governance · Security policy · Contributor Covenant 2.1 applies.
