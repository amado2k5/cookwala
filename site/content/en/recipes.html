V0 and V1

{{s:recipes.v0_note}}

The nine recipes written for the standard are V1: typed operations with parameters, end conditions, timeouts, hazards and critical control points, validated and semantically checked; still not simulated and not field-verified. Every document says which it is.

Where the recipes come from

The imported recipes are the fifi.cooking collection: the family archive of Dr. Fatma Alkawokgy and four further collections (Chef Teta, Osool El Tahy, Egyptian Cooking by Samia Abdennour, and the Fatma Abu Haty channel), each credited in the document. The family archive is published under CC BY 4.0; the other collections are published as credited facts with the licence under review by the founder, and say so. Nothing is claimed about any recipe that the document does not carry.

fifi.cooking publishes its recipes as open JSON files under fifi.cooking/data, which its website and its Android, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, Fire TV and Samsung TV apps all read. The technology behind fifi.cooking.

Every recipe is a JSON document at /v1/recipes/<id>.cookwala.json, with text sidecars per language at /v1/recipes/<id>/text/<lang>.json, a paged index at /v1/index/<lang>/<page>.json and a signed-ready manifest at /v1/manifest.json. The export tool and its report are in the repository (tools/export_fifi.py, recipes/REPORT.md).

Add your own

This index isn't only fifi.cooking's: anyone can add a recipe. By pull request, by a GitHub issue if you don't use git, by the cookwala submit command, or by API. Your own recipe or someone else's, credited either way, entering as V0 (described, not machine-verified) like every recipe here until it's reviewed further.
