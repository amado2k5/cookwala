Cookwala / Privacy

Privacy policy

Cookwala has no accounts, no sign-in, no cookies of its own and no analytics. Its recipe data is public and contains no personal data. Sections 1 to 5 state, as plainly as we can, the five things a privacy policy should cover; sections 6 to 10 cover who is responsible, legal bases, transfers, your rights by jurisdiction, and children. Last updated 11 October 2026.

1. Categories of personal data we collect, process or return

  • Collected by Cookwala: none. Cookwala's own code does not ask for, receive in a stored form, or keep your name, email, account, location, device identifiers, health information or conversation history.
  • Processed in passing: the request you or your AI assistant sends to https://mcp.cookwala.ai (a search term, filters, a recipe id). It is answered from public files and discarded; it is not written to a database, log or file by Cookwala. Search terms are not personal data unless you type personal data into them, so please do not.
  • Returned by the tools: recipe data only: titles, ingredients, steps, nutrition and cost estimates, and diet, allergen and diabetic labels about recipes. No tool returns data about a person, and responses carry no session, trace, request or account identifiers.
  • Technical data seen by hosting providers: like any web host, Cloudflare (which runs mcp.cookwala.ai) and GitHub (which serves cookwala.ai) handle the network address and request details needed to deliver pages and protect against abuse. Cookwala does not receive or store that data. Cookwala does not enable request logging on its Worker.
  • Email: if you write to eat@cookwala.ai, the message is forwarded to a private mailbox, so your address and what you write reach the maintainer.

2. Purposes

The only purpose is to answer your request with recipe data and to keep the service available. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, tracking, training or sale. If a per-address limit is switched on for the open API, the address is used only as a short-lived counter key, at the host, to slow down abuse.

3. Categories of recipients

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting of the API and MCP endpoint) and GitHub, Inc. (hosting of the website and the repository), as infrastructure providers under their own policies: Cloudflare, GitHub.
  • Your AI assistant's provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) sees your conversation under its own policy. Cookwala only receives the tool call the assistant makes.
  • mcprush.com counts calls made through its gateway to the token-protected /mcp route. This is the one opt-in exception; the open route (/open/mcp and /api/*) and the npm package are not tracked.
  • No other third party receives any data, and none is sold or shared for advertising.

4. Retention

Cookwala keeps nothing about requests, so there is nothing to retain or delete. Providers keep the technical data described above for the periods in their own policies. Emails to eat@cookwala.ai are kept in the maintainer's mailbox until they are deleted on request or no longer needed.

5. Your controls

  • There is no account to close and no profile to edit or export.
  • You decide what you type: do not include personal or medical information in a question. Cookwala does not need it.
  • To avoid contacting the hosted endpoint at all, run the server on your machine with npx -y @cookwala/mcp; it only reads the public static files on cookwala.ai.
  • To have an email you sent deleted, write to eat@cookwala.ai or open an issue at GitHub.

6. Who is responsible

Cookwala is run by its founder, Ahmed Abdel Aal, who is the controller of the little personal data described here (in Canadian terms, the person accountable for it) and the person in charge of the protection of personal information under Quebec's Law 25. Contact: eat@cookwala.ai.

7. Legal bases and cookies

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies: technical data handled by our hosts is processed on the basis of legitimate interests (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) in delivering and protecting the Service; emails you send are processed to answer you (legitimate interests, or steps you ask us to take). Cookwala sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for one thing, remembering the light or dark theme you pick (key cw-theme), which never leaves your device; this is strictly necessary for a feature you ask for, so no consent banner is needed. We do not track you, so Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are treated as already honoured. We do not sell or “share” personal information as those words are used in California law, and we do not make automated decisions about people.

8. International transfers

GitHub, Cloudflare and mcprush process requests on servers around the world, including in the United States. Where the GDPR or UK law applies, these providers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension or on standard contractual clauses, as described in their policies. Emails are kept in a mailbox hosted in Canada or the United States.

9. Your rights and where to complain

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to access, correct or delete it, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict processing, to portability, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. These come from laws including Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA and other U.S. state privacy laws, and Brazil's LGPD. Since we keep nothing but emails, most requests concern an email you sent. Write to eat@cookwala.ai; we answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm the request is yours. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information, your EU data protection authority, the UK ICO or the California Privacy Protection Agency.

10. Children

Cookwala does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The Cooking with Kids recipes are content about cooking with children, not a service that children sign up for. If a child has emailed us, a parent or guardian can ask us to delete the message.

Health-related labels

Allergen, diet and diabetic labels describe recipes, not people. They are screens from ingredient names and reviewed claims, never guarantees and never medical advice. Cookwala does not ask for, receive or store anyone's health information.

Changes and contact

This policy changes only by a public commit in the repository, so its history is visible. Questions: eat@cookwala.ai. Security problems: security.txt. See also the terms of use and the disclaimer.
