Cookwala / Privacy

Privacy policy

Cookwala has no accounts, no sign-in, no cookies of its own and no analytics. Its recipe data is public and contains no personal data. This policy states, as plainly as it can, the five things a privacy policy should cover. Last updated 8 October 2026.

1. Categories of personal data we collect, process or return

  • Collected by Cookwala: none. Cookwala's own code does not ask for, receive in a stored form, or keep your name, email, account, location, device identifiers, health information or conversation history.
  • Processed in passing: the request you or your AI assistant sends to https://mcp.cookwala.ai (a search term, filters, a recipe id). It is answered from public files and discarded; it is not written to a database, log or file by Cookwala. Search terms are not personal data unless you type personal data into them, so please do not.
  • Returned by the tools: recipe data only: titles, ingredients, steps, nutrition and cost estimates, and diet, allergen and diabetic labels about recipes. No tool returns data about a person, and responses carry no session, trace, request or account identifiers.
  • Technical data seen by hosting providers: like any web host, Cloudflare (which runs mcp.cookwala.ai) and GitHub (which serves cookwala.ai) handle the network address and request details needed to deliver pages and protect against abuse. Cookwala does not receive or store that data. Cookwala does not enable request logging on its Worker.
  • Email: if you write to eat@cookwala.ai, the message is forwarded to a private mailbox, so your address and what you write reach the maintainer.

2. Purposes

The only purpose is to answer your request with recipe data and to keep the service available. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, tracking, training or sale. If a per-address limit is switched on for the open API, the address is used only as a short-lived counter key, at the host, to slow down abuse.

3. Categories of recipients

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting of the API and MCP endpoint) and GitHub, Inc. (hosting of the website and the repository), as infrastructure providers under their own policies: Cloudflare, GitHub.
  • Your AI assistant's provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) sees your conversation under its own policy. Cookwala only receives the tool call the assistant makes.
  • mcprush.com counts calls made through its gateway to the token-protected /mcp route. This is the one opt-in exception; the open route (/open/mcp and /api/*) and the npm package are not tracked.
  • No other third party receives any data, and none is sold or shared for advertising.

4. Retention

Cookwala keeps nothing about requests, so there is nothing to retain or delete. Providers keep the technical data described above for the periods in their own policies. Emails to eat@cookwala.ai are kept in the maintainer's mailbox until they are deleted on request or no longer needed.

5. Your controls

  • There is no account to close and no profile to edit or export.
  • You decide what you type: do not include personal or medical information in a question. Cookwala does not need it.
  • To avoid contacting the hosted endpoint at all, run the server on your machine with npx -y @cookwala/mcp; it only reads the public static files on cookwala.ai.
  • To have an email you sent deleted, write to eat@cookwala.ai or open an issue at GitHub.

Health-related labels

Allergen, diet and diabetic labels describe recipes, not people. They are screens from ingredient names and reviewed claims, never guarantees and never medical advice. Cookwala does not ask for, receive or store anyone's health information.

Changes and contact

This policy changes only by a public commit in the repository, so its history is visible. Questions: eat@cookwala.ai. Security problems: security.txt. See also the terms of use.
