Within a minute: why Cookwala matters to you, and one thing you can do today.

Robots are learning to move. Nobody had written down how to cook.

Home robots are shipping and kitchen robots are taking orders. Each maker writes its own closed recipes, mostly from a few cuisines, and decides alone what "simmer" or "done" means.

Unsafe food causes about 600 million illnesses a year measured · WHO. About 13 % of food is lost before it reaches a shop measured · FAO, while about 733 million people faced hunger in 2023 measured · SOFI 2024.

Cookwala is the shared, open layer: one way to describe a recipe that a person, a food bank, an oven or a humanoid can read, check and cook safely, in every cuisine, with the cook's name on it. Why this, why now · Impact, with sources

One loop

  1. 1

    Describe

    Write a recipe as steps with end conditions and hazards. Hash and sign it.

    cookwala hash recipe.json
  2. 2

    Check

    Validate it, then dry-run it against a device before cooking.

    cookwala dryrun recipe.json --device oven.json
  3. 3

    Cook

    The device runs it through the Core API or ROS 2, keeping every step inside its safe band.

    POST /v1/executions
  4. 4

    Learn

    With consent, the log becomes a trace and a training example for robots.

    cookwala export lerobot …

Safe by design, verifiable by anyone

Safety is enforced on the device, not in the cloud. No recipe, agent, message or extension can raise a device's limits, and a local stop works without a network. AI agents act only under a signed mandate and treat every text field as data, never as instructions.

Every recipe and record can be hashed and signed, keys can be revoked, and event logs carry witnessed checkpoints. The tests that prove it are public: {{stat:conformanceVectors}} vectors.

Why we do this

Help end hunger

Food that would be thrown away reaching a plate safely; programs that can compare their numbers; later, farms and stores that plan to need. Hunger has many causes: poverty, conflict, climate, prices, policy. Cookwala's part is real and partial, and we say which.

Make people healthier

Critical control points a device must meet; hot-holding and cooling it must record; rule packs for sodium, sugar, fat, fruit and vegetables and care for children and older people, reviewable by dietitians and food-safety officers.

Put robots to work for people

Robots that cook every cuisine safely, learn with consent and credit, serve people who cannot cook for themselves, and give human time back, with a labour voice at the table where this is governed.
