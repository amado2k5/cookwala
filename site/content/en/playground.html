Envelope explorer: when does a simmer stop being a simmer?

Pick an operation and drag the points of a temperature trace. The checker is the same function the conformance vectors test: the warm-up is allowed, then the medium must stay inside the band; a recipe target, once reached, must hold. Water bands shift with altitude.

Simulators, explained for non-experts

Four deterministic models, each playable with the protocol on and off, each labelled illustrative model. They do not predict the future; they show what follows from the assumptions written on each page, and every assumption can be changed.

A home

A family asks a robot for dinner. Watch the request travel through providers, approvals, interruptions and a closure; switch the protocol off and see the same evening with the robot alone.

Play →

Two cities, one month

Farms, a wholesale hub, shops, 1,500 homes per city, trucks, garbage and energy. Robots alone push waste upstream; with shared plans, losses before homes fall.

Play →

One country, one year

Six provinces adopting at different speeds, seasons, a relief program. Rescue covers about 5 % of need in the mixed rollout: real, and not enough.

Play →

The world, five years

Ten regions, three robot scenarios, protocol on or off. Hunger is where robots are not; the protocol helps countries without robots.
