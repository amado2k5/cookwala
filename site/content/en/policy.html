Something concrete to point to

Cooking machines are arriving under regulations written for appliances and software separately. Food donation runs on spreadsheets that differ by site. An open, royalty-free standard gives regulators and programs a reviewable technical basis: refusal before heat, safety limits on the device, signed records, anonymous incident reporting, a conformance suite anyone can run, and a donation data standard with no personal data.

Model language, four sections

  • A. Food donation: no personal data; aggregates and small numbers; a cold-chain record at every custody transfer; retention; written acceptance rules reviewed by a professional; an open format; good-faith donor protection.
  • B. Cooking machines: refusal before operation; local safety limits no update can raise; a local stop (0.5 s motion, 1 s heat); text is data; agents under mandate; verifiable records with no personal data; recall feeds; anonymous incident reporting; conformance reports before any mark.
  • C. Household data held by domestic robots: stored on the device, erasable, leaving only as derived constraints or with withdrawable consent; no behavioural score; no inferred economic status; inferred facts never the sole basis of a safety decision.
  • D. Procurement language for school-meal and relief programs.

Written to match what the standard actually does; not legal advice; each jurisdiction's drafters fit terms to local law.

What this is not

Not a certification: the path to one is described and no certifier is engaged. Not medical advice. Not a product. Not endorsed by any government or agency. Not a claim to end hunger: hunger is driven by poverty, conflict, climate and prices, and this standard contributes through waste, safety and coordination.

Status and governance

Core 0.2 is a draft under public review. Decisions are made in public with written reasons; a steering committee with seats for food banks, a low- or middle-income country, labour, privacy and dietetics takes over once there are independent adopters, under published numbers (quorum, windows, employer cap); the specification moves to a neutral foundation with a patent non-assertion pledge.
