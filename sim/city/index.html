Robots alone vs robots + the Cookwala protocol

Same city, same people, same robot-cook homes, same seed. Only the protocol differs. Off = robots order and cook on their own (vendor apps, no shared plans with stores, farms or delivery, no surplus rescue, no energy-saver modes). On = demand signals, consolidated delivery, surplus rescue, reusable packaging, energy-saver cooking. Use each city's switch above to play either version.
