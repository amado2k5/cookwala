# Scope

This Working Group develops the Cookwala specifications: an open, royalty-free interchange format
and safety semantics for cooking by people, kitchen appliances, kitchen robots and software agents.
The Scope, for the purposes of the Community Specification License 1.0 ([LICENSE-SPEC.md](LICENSE-SPEC.md)),
is:

1. the Core specification (`docs/CORE.md`) and its JSON Schemas (`schemas/`), vocabularies
   (`vocab/`), API descriptions (`api/`), conformance vectors (`conformance/`) and bindings
   (`bindings/`);
2. the profiles and their specifications (`docs/*-PROFILE.md`, `docs/HOUSEHOLD-CONTEXT.md`,
   `docs/REGISTRY.md`, `docs/FEDERATION.md`, `docs/CERTIFICATION.md`, `docs/KITCHENS-AND-FLEETS.md`,
   `docs/SUPPLY-SIGNALS.md`, `docs/MISSION.md`, `docs/PROTOCOL.md`, `profiles/`);
3. the RFCs in `rfcs/` once accepted;

including the data formats, document kinds, hashing and signing rules, refusal and safety
semantics, event-log and checkpoint rules, conformance and certification report formats, and
the registry and federation rules those documents describe.

Out of Scope: the reference implementations and SDKs (licensed under Apache-2.0, which carries its
own patent licence), the recipe data, the website, and any device's own motion control, hardware
or firmware.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
