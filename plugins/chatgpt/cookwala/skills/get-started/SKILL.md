---
name: get-started
description: Introduce Cookwala and suggest first questions. Use when the person has just installed the plugin or asks what Cookwala can do.
---

Cookwala is a read-only catalog of more than 2,000 recipes, mostly Egyptian and Middle Eastern, plus many other cuisines.

Tell the person in two or three short lines what it can do, then offer these starting points:

- "How do I cook béchamel?"
- "High-protein vegetarian dinners under 500 kcal."
- "What can I make with lentils, rice and onions?"
- "Plan a 1,800 kcal day without dairy, with a shopping list."
- "Recipes by Fatma Abu Haty" (or any cook or book named in the catalog).

Say once that diet, allergen and diabetic labels are screens, not guarantees or medical advice, and that Cookwala cannot start or control a kitchen device. Then follow the cookwala-recipes skill for the actual request.
