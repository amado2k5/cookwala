# Cookwala Patent Non-Assertion Pledge

fifi.cooking and every contributor who signs the contribution agreement pledge not to
assert any patent claim they own or control against any person or organization for making,
using, selling, offering for sale, importing or distributing an implementation of the
Cookwala specifications (schemas, APIs, protocols and vocabularies in this repository), to
the extent the claim is necessarily infringed by implementing them.

This pledge does not apply to anyone who first asserts a patent claim against an
implementation of the Cookwala specifications, or against fifi.cooking or a contributor
over their Cookwala work. For that party, the pledge ends.

This pledge is in addition to the patent license in the Apache License 2.0 that covers the
code in this repository.

[Community Specification License 1.0](LICENSE-SPEC.md) (Scope: [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md); notices and
acceptance: [NOTICES.md](NOTICES.md)). Its section 2 grants royalty-free patent licences to
Necessary Claims from every Contributor and every accepting Licensee, section 2.4 binds future
owners and assignees of those claims, and section 2.3 governs withdrawal. Contributors accept it by
signing off their commits ([CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md](CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md)).
The initial Contributor is the founder (GitHub @amado2k5), acting for fifi.cooking; the legal
person behind that name will be stated here when counsel has reviewed this file. This pledge
remains in force alongside the License.
