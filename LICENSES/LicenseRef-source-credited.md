# LicenseRef-source-credited

**What this identifier means.** A document marked `LicenseRef-source-credited` publishes only
**structured facts** extracted from a credited source: the dish name in each language, the
ingredient lines with parsed quantities, servings, times, allergen and food-class inferences,
nutrition and cost estimates, and a link to the source. It contains **no step text, no notes and no
other copyrighted expression of the source**. The source's own licence governs the source; this
identifier grants nothing in it.

**What you may do with the facts.** The structured facts and the Cookwala document structure are
offered under CC0 1.0 to the extent the publisher holds rights in them; where a jurisdiction
protects compilations of facts, attribution to the named source and to fifi.cooking is requested.

**Why this exists.** Four fifi.cooking collections derive from cookbooks or a video channel whose
rights are not yet confirmed in writing (`tools/export_fifi.collections.json`). Until they are,
those collections are exported with `text: facts`, and this identifier marks the result.
When a collection's rights are confirmed, it moves to `CC-BY-4.0` or to the licence the rights
holder grants, and the step text is published.
